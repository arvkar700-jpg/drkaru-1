    Have a PDF statement? Open it, select all (Ctrl/⌘ + A), copy, and paste here. Each line should start with a date.
    
        
        
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    2Review each subscription
    
      
    Tell KaruScan when you last used each one. Review candidates before deciding what to keep, cancel or investigate.
    
    
    
    
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    3How cancelling works
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Cancel at the source
    
        
    Open “How to cancel” on any row. KaruScan links to that company's billing page and lists the steps. Cancelling there stops the next charge. Bank blocks alone often don't, because companies can re-bill a new card.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Dispute what you don't recognise
    
        
    Mark a charge “I don't recognise this” and KaruScan writes a dispute letter for your bank's card services team, asking them to reverse the charges, block the merchant and advise on replacing your card.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Check the next statement
    
        
    After you mark something cancelled, add next month's statement. If the same company charges you again, KaruScan flags it in red so you can claim a refund.
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      Settings, backup and report
      
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
    
          
          
          
          
        
    
        
        
    
        
    KaruScan stores all imported payments and decisions unencrypted in this browser. Anyone with access to this browser profile, or scripts on the same website origin, may read them. Backups and copied text contain financial data. Storage can be cleared by the browser; export a backup before relying on saved decisions. Use “Copy backup” and keep the text somewhere safe if you want to move to another device.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    KaruScan cannot sign in to your bank or your app accounts, so the final “Cancel” click is always yours. It reads your statement in this browser, finds the subscriptions, tells you which to stop, takes you to the right page, and drafts the emails. Billing pages change from time to time. If a link has moved, open the company's site and look under Settings → Billing or Subscription.
